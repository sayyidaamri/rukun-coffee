10 years walking together

Every July reminds us of RUKUN's small journey. It began with a single counter in a Kemang alley; today RUKUN greets neighbors in many cities and once carried the warmth of coffee together across the sea. With more than 50 stores, our baristas are ready to welcome every neighbor, every day. Growing is not only about store counts or sales figures, but about showing up for one another, looking out for each other and lending a hand. These ten years invite us to cherish every greeting and every moment together. Our hope is simple: that with every step, RUKUN keeps passing kindness around. Let's celebrate a new year warmly and together. Thank you for walking with us, neighbor.
